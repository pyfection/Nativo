# Privacy policy

_Last updated: October 8, 2026_

Nativo is a free, community-run archive for endangered languages. This policy
explains what personal data the website and the Nativo apps for Android and
iOS handle, why, and what you can do about it.

## What we collect and why

**Your account.** Email address, username and password (stored only as a
secure hash). We need them to run your account, sign you in, and send emails
you ask for (confirming your address, resetting your password). We don't send
newsletters or marketing.

**What you contribute.** Words, translations, texts, spellings, links between
words and texts, votes, suggestions and **voice recordings**. Nativo exists to
preserve languages, so contributions are published in the archive together
with your username. A voice recording is personal data: anyone can listen to
published recordings.

**Your languages.** Which languages you joined, your level in each, and your
editing and reviewing permissions there.

**Learning progress.** Which words you know, which ones you looked up, and the
texts you finished, so Learn can suggest what to practise next. Only you see it.

**API tokens.** If you create a token for scripts or AI assistants, we store
its name, when it was last used, and a hash of the token (never the token
itself).

**Technical data.** When your device talks to our servers, the servers
see its IP address and the requested page. We use it to deliver the page, to
limit abuse (for example too many login attempts), and in short-lived server
logs for fixing errors.

**On your device.** The app keeps your sign-in, your settings (language,
theme), copies of pages you opened (so they work offline) and changes you made
offline that weren't sent yet. This stays in your browser or app storage and is
wiped when you sign out (unsent changes stay until they're sent or you delete
your account).

The microphone is only used while you press Record, and only after you allow
it. We don't use analytics, advertising, tracking cookies or social media
plugins, and we don't sell or share your data with anyone for their own use.

## Legal basis

- Running your account and publishing your contributions, as you asked:
  performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
- Server logs, abuse protection, and keeping contributions in the archive after
  an account is deleted: our legitimate interest in a secure, lasting language
  archive (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

## Who processes data for us

Nativo runs on these services. They store or process data only on our behalf:

- **Fly.io** — runs the Nativo servers (United States).
- **Neon** — hosts the database.
- **Tigris** — stores voice recordings.
- **Cloudflare** — delivers the website.
- **[EMAIL PROVIDER]** — sends account emails.

Some of these are based in, or store data in, the United States. Transfers rely
on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard
contractual clauses.

## How long we keep it

- Account data: until you delete your account.
- Learning progress, language memberships and API tokens: until you delete your
  account (or the token).
- Contributions: they stay in the archive, also after you delete your account.
  They are then shown under an anonymous name instead of your username.
- Server logs: up to 30 days.

## Deleting your account

Signed in, open the user menu and choose **Delete account** (or go to
[/account/delete](/account/delete)). This removes your email address, username,
password, language memberships, learning progress and API tokens right away.
Your contributions stay in the archive under an anonymous name like
"deleted-user-3f2a…".

If you also want your **voice recordings** or other contributions removed,
write to us at {{contact_email}} before or after deleting your account, and we
will remove them.

## Your rights

You have the right to access your data, have it corrected or deleted, restrict
or object to its use, and receive it in a portable format. Write to us at the
address above. You can also complain to a data protection supervisory
authority, for example the one where you live.

## Children

Nativo is open to learners of all ages, but you need to be at least 16 to
create an account, or have permission from a parent or guardian.

## Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change
matters for how your data is used, we'll tell signed-in users in the app.
